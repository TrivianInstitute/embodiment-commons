# Choose a practice

[Home](../README.md)

All seven practices are optional, educational, and unpiloted. Each includes purpose, duration, context, instructions, modifications, reflection, and cautions. No special equipment, AI account, touch, or breath manipulation is required.

| Practice | Time | Context |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Arriving before an AI interaction](01-arriving.md) | 2–4 minutes | Before a writing, planning, or learning exchange |
| [A short post-screen sensory reset](02-sensory-reset.md) | 2–5 minutes | After digital work, reading, or concentrated near-focus activity |
| [An embodied pause before consequential decisions](03-decision-pause.md) | 2–5 minutes | Before a non-urgent choice affecting yourself or others |
| [Checking in during extended collaboration](04-collaboration-check-in.md) | 3–5 minutes | During a long AI conversation, study group, writing session, or meeting |
| [A movement transition between cognitive tasks](05-movement-transition.md) | 2–4 minutes | Between meetings, lessons, or focused tasks |
| [Noticing bodily cues during uncertainty](06-uncertainty.md) | 4–6 minutes | A low-stakes reflection on an unresolved question |
| [Orienting to place after digital focus](07-environmental-orientation.md) | 3–7 minutes | After prolonged digital work or before entering a shared space |

Choose one that fits the situation, not a prescribed sequence. An external-attention or purely reflective alternative is available in each practice. No practice grants a credential or requires a report of benefit.
